# Crossroads of Identity

Private literary and adaptation repository for **Crossroads of Identity**, a seven-book universe under EstibanCreations.

## Canon hierarchy
1. Original/published manuscript prose
2. Revised book outlines
3. Character Histories and world notes
4. Film/series adaptation documents
5. VisionWeaver training and production mappings

Adaptation work may expand staging, visual language, performance, environment, and production design, but may not silently rewrite book canon.
