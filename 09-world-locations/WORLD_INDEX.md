## Adaptation rule
Before locking any location for film/series production, identify whether it is:
- `MANUSCRIPT_CANON`
- `WORLD_NOTE_CANON`
- `ADAPTATION_EXPANSION`
- `VISIONWEAVER_TRAINING_REFERENCE`

Production design may enrich a canonical place but must not silently convert later adaptation inventions into book canon.
