# Crossroads of Identity — Film & Series Adaptation Rules

## First principle
The **books are the first scripts to follow**.

## Canon hierarchy
1. Original/published manuscript prose
2. Revised book outlines
3. Character Histories and world notes
4. Adaptation documents
5. VisionWeaver training/mapping

## Allowed adaptation work
Screen development may:
- restructure scene order for cinematic pacing when the underlying story meaning is preserved;
- expand visual staging, performance, environment, costume, sound and camera language;
- build character/location continuity bibles from manuscript evidence;
- create shot and scene packages for VisionWeaver/Runway;
- identify book-to-season or book-to-film structures after canon mapping.

## Not allowed without explicit creator approval
- changing character identities or core relationships;
- moving the story's world away from manuscript continuity;
- replacing manuscript events with later AI-generated material;
- importing `This Is Your Life` cosmology or characters;
- treating the mislabeled `This Is Your Life` production bible as authority over the novels.
